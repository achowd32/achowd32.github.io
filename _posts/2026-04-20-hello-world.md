## Writing posts

Each post is a Markdown file with a bit of YAML front matter at the top:

```yaml
---
title: "Your post title"
date: 2026-04-21
---
```

Everything below the front matter is standard Markdown. Headings, lists, links, images, and code blocks all work out of the box.
